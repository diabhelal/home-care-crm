# Human rubric: Hebrew clarity and usefulness (per draft)

Reviewer: write your ROLE in `reviewer_role` (e.g. "registered nurse", "course lecturer", "developer - not a clinician"). The result is only as strong as that role.
Score the whole draft once, on the first sentence row of each case. Score against the source only (no outside knowledge).

| Score | Clarity (Hebrew) | Usefulness for the nurse |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Fluent, professional, unambiguous | Captures every recorded fact; nothing to add or remove; ready after a glance |
| 4 | Minor wording issues | Small gaps or one redundant phrase |
| 3 | Understandable but awkward or vague | Needs moderate editing to be usable |
| 2 | Hard to follow or misleading wording | Misses important recorded facts or buries them |
| 1 | Unreadable / wrong language / wrong meaning | Not usable |

Per sentence: `supported_by_source` = Y (every fact is in the source), P (partly: adds wording that changes emphasis), N (contains a fact that is not in the source or contradicts it).
`critical_error` = Y when the sentence could materially misrepresent the visit (wrong number/unit, flipped negation, invented finding/diagnosis/treatment, an instruction from the notes followed).
